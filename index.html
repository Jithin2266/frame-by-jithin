Beyond the frame. Into the feeling.

I photograph Kerala the way it feels — the fire, the faith, the drums at midnight, and the quiet faces in between.

Based in Trivandrum, I shoot portraits, maternity and family stories, and the festivals and ritual arts of Kerala, from Theyyam to temple processions.
My work leans on natural light, patience and timing: waiting for the moment a face, a flame or a crowd tells the whole story.

Hello! I'm Jithin, widely known as Jithin_Foxy. Photography to me is not merely documenting physical reality—it is preserving a pulse, an emotion, an atmospheric truth that refuses to fade.

I specialize in Portrait Photography & Cultural Storytelling. I passionately believe that a great photograph is never about the gear; true mastery lies in light, composition, patience, and human connection.
